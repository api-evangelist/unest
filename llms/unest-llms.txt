# UNest

> UNest is a fintech app that helps parents and families save and invest for children's futures through UTMA/UGMA custodial accounts, 529 college savings plans, and term life insurance.

## About
- SEC-registered investment adviser (UNest Advisers, LLC)
- Brokerage services through UNest Securities, LLC (FINRA/SIPC member)
- Clearing through Apex Clearing Corporation

## Products
- [Investment Accounts](https://unest.co/pricing): UTMA/UGMA custodial accounts for children
- [Life Insurance](https://unest.co/life-insurance): Affordable term life insurance through Ladder
- [College Savings](https://unest.co/insights): Educational content for parents
